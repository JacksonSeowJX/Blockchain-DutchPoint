Evidence 12 - Bid protection: an on-chain minimum for bids (A1, structural fix), and contract errors decoded for transactions.
Recorded 2026-10-26. Tests committed failing in cdadc79, contract and frontend in 5b7be42;
error-decoding test committed failing in 689c41f, fixed in 2765efa.

The first fix for independent-review finding A1 (fe44d91, evidence 11) made the quote honest: "If fully
accepted, at least". This change makes it true. contributeWithMinimum(minTokens) reverts with
MinimumTokensNotMet(minimum, actual) unless the accepted part of the bid buys at least minTokens at the price
when it is mined. The final price can only be at or below that price, so the claim for the bid is at least
minTokens. contribute() and plain ETH transfers keep their behaviour (no minimum), so the F1-F4 regression
tests and the crowd-out proof of concept are untouched.

What the new Solidity tests check:
- Fuzz: an earlier bid of random size, a quote at a random moment, the bid mined after a random delay, and
  a random minimum of 0-120% of the quote. Either the bid reverts with exactly
  MinimumTokensNotMet(minimum, tokens) and changes nothing, or it is accepted and the eventual claim is at
  least the minimum. A fully accepted bid can only fall short of a minimum above its quote.
- Invariant: protected bids join the random sequences (bids, time jumps, finalize, claims, withdrawals,
  forced ETH, holder burns). Every actor's allocation (tokensOwed, at the current, clearing or final price)
  always covers the summed minimums of their accepted protected bids. No protected bid may revert for any
  other reason.

Finding (C1): `revert undefined` above. ethers v6 decodes a custom error into `error.revert` only for read
calls. For a transaction, the raw revert data is in `error.data` (gas-estimate failure) or nested in
`error.error.data` (node rejection), so describeError, which only read `revert.name`, never matched any entry
in its table of contract errors. A bid cancelled by its minimum would have shown "Transaction failed.", and a
bid after sell-out "could not coalesce error". This predates bid protection: it affected every transaction
since the frontend was written (second pass).
