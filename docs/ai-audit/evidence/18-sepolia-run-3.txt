== 3. D3 and D4 in the recording
The page showed the final clearing price as 0.000057 ETH (the price paid was 0.0000576), bid 2's price as
0.000079 (0.0000792), and the dust button as "Burn 1 units of rounding dust". The demo video's captions give
the exact figures.

Origin: formatPrice() cut every price to six decimals in Codex's first version (0a75a78,
src/lib/format.ts); Claude Code's frontend rebuild kept it (c689e3c), and the redesign's large price figure
(priceFigure, 895079d) did the same. The local sale's prices (0.02 -> 0.005) never need more than six. The
dust label is from c689e3c.
